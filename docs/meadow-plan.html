Rabbit Meadow Plan

How the slideshow's jade rabbit and its little world grow over a long afternoon of looping, so the slides are readable at first and stay fresh later. Everything on the timeline below is built; tweak the times here and in PHASES in slides.js.

The idea

Complexity is a distraction, but a welcome one once people are bored of the slides. The rabbit waits until the room has seen every slide, then comes and goes, staying a little longer each time, until late in the afternoon it stays. One new thing at a time, so there's always something new coming up.

The guard. The rabbit doesn't come out until every slide has been on screen for 5 s in one go: a full autoplay loop, or someone presenting at a normal pace. Flicking through quickly doesn't count. It's remembered for the day, so a reload doesn't make the room sit through another loop.

On and off stage. After that it comes out for a visit, plays, and hops off again. When its time is up it finishes what it's doing first (a nap, a carrot, a greeting) and leaves at its next decision; the friend goes home and the insects fly off. While it's away the ground rests. Visits get longer and breaks shorter over the afternoon, until from 16:10 it stays.

The rabbit button in the slideshow controls (or R) turns it off for anyone who'd rather not have it; on by default. Play and pause no longer affect it.

Everything follows the event clock. On Saturday 24 Oct it's the real time in Stockholm, so a pause, a reload or a second screen never puts it out of step. Before 14:00 (setup) it's at the start, after 16:40 it's full. On any other day (people browsing the site) the clock is play time counted from 14:00.

Phase-in timeline

Event clock. Gold = the rabbits and visitors, green = the ecosystem, dashed = the quiet stretch and ideas not built yet. “Out / away” is how long a visit and a break last.

  1. 14:00

    Just the rabbit built

    After the first clean loop it hops in from a side. Rests, grooms, sniffs, stands up to look around, turns its head to face the room now and then, short hops, strolls, sploots, naps with z's. No carrot on its first visit: digging one up is saved for a later one. No butterflies, no binkies yet. Out 60–90 s, away 120–150 s.

  2. 14:10

    A butterfly built

    One butterfly at a time, flitting around the rabbit. The rabbit watches it with its eyes, looks up when it's right overhead, chases it with its chin up and binkies when it dips low. Out 90–120 s, away 90–120 s.

  3. 14:15

    A friend visits built

    A smaller cream rabbit with brown ears and a brown back patch, only while the jade rabbit is out (it often tags along early in a visit). The two touch noses, play follow-the-leader, groom each other or sploot side by side.

  4. 14:25

    More butterflies built

    Up to 2 at once, about twice as often. The rabbit is out most of the time now: out 120–150 s, away 60–80 s.

  5. 14:30

    Quiet stretch, until 15:30

    Opening, games and performances, Kahoot. No milestones. The rabbit drops right back to a rare visit (out 60–90 s, away 8–10 min); while it's away, a butterfly or the odd bee drifts through now and then, so a slideshow left running has a little life without pulling eyes from the stage.

  6. 15:30

    Grass grows in built

    Marks the new part of the afternoon with the feast. Low grass rises out of the floor over about 2.5 minutes, and the rabbit starts coming out more again: out 2–3 min, away 60–90 s.

  7. 15:40

    Carrots grow in the ground built

    Digging on the spot stops. One carrot at a time sprouts among the grass and ripens over 50 s; a rabbit that sees a ripe one goes and digs it up. Out 3–4 min, away 40–60 s.

  8. 15:50

    Friends share built

    When one rabbit pulls up a carrot the other may nibble the other end; it goes 1.8× faster. Friend visits every 6–9 min, lasting 1.5–2.5 min. Out 4–5 min, away 30–45 s.

  9. 16:00

    First flowers built

    Up to 2 flowers: sprout, bud, bloom about a minute, wilt, gone. Butterflies now look for blooms, land, sip and move on; rabbits sometimes smell a flower.

  10. 16:10

    More flowers built

    Up to 4 flowers and 2 carrots in the ground. The rabbit stays out from here.

  11. 16:20

    A bee built

    One bee: fast zig-zags, hovers over flowers instead of landing. A rabbit flattens its ears and hops away if it buzzes at its face.

  12. 16:40

    Full meadow built

    Up to 8 flowers (fewer on narrow screens), 3 butterflies, 2 bees; the friend visits every 4–6 min.

  13. 17:00+

    Later surprises ideas

    Fireflies around the flowers while a rabbit naps · falling autumn leaves · a short rain shower that speeds up growth · a dropped mooncake to nibble · a slow snail the rabbit sniffs.

When the plan changes (a milestone, the quiet stretch starting or ending), a visit or break that's longer than the new plan allows is cut to fit, so the rabbit doesn't sit out a 10-minute quiet-stretch break after 15:30.

Rules that keep it calm

  • Nothing until every slide has been seen for 5 s; then short visits first.
  • Everything lives in the bottom strip of the stage; insects stay within about 160 rabbit-units of the floor.
  • Caps scale with stage width, so a phone never gets crowded.
  • The rabbit button turns it all off; play and pause only drive the slides and the music.
  • The site's membership-box rabbit stays at the base level (rabbit, carrots, the odd butterfly), with no phases and no friend.

What's in the code

rabbit.js is one file for every rabbit on the site and the slideshow. The world (grass, flowers, carrots in the ground, insects) is shared; each rabbit is its own Bunny with its own state, cooldowns and look.

ElementBehaviour and numbers
RabbitRest fidgets (blink, glance, ears, groom, sniff, stand up, loaf); hops with crouch, arc and landing squash; max 6 hops then rest; strolls remember their goal; sploot every 20–40 s at most; nap 8–15 s every 1.5–2.5 min with a stretch; binky every 15–35 s at most. Looks: eyes follow what it watches (rolled well up for things overhead); a butterfly right overhead gets a chin-up look; when chasing, chin and eyes stay on the butterfly; now and then it turns its head to face the room (body stays side-on; both ears and eyes, a little smile, still blinking, glancing, twitching ears, nose and mouth) for 2–3 s. The roll-over is gone.
Friend rabbit86% size, cream fur, brown ears and back patch. Arrives from the nearer side, greets with a 2.4 s nose touch, then plays every 14–24 s at most: follow-the-leader (½ of the time), grooming, side-by-side sploot. Shares carrots when share is on (65% of the time, within 320 units).
Carrots on the spotDig 1.4–2 s, pull 0.9 s, eat 5.5 s (1.8× faster when shared), 10–20 s between. On the site, grabbing mid-meal leaves the carrot on the floor (up to 5).
ButterfliesRound wings (pointed and swallowtail still drawn in code), six colours. Without flowers they flit around the rabbit; with flowers they seek blooms, sip 3–7 s, visit 2–4, leave after 30–55 s. A rabbit chases one at a time for 8–14 s, switching every 4–7 s.
GrassPatches every ~150 units, 26–70 wide, blades 5–14 high, sway; ~22% of blades in front of the rabbits. Grows in over 150 s.
FlowersDaisy, pink daisy, star, tulip, bellflower. Grow 12–18 s, bloom 45–75 s, wilt 7–10 s.
Carrots in the groundRipen in 50 s, a new one every 20–35 s while there's room, spotted within 260 units.
BeesNeed blooms. 150 units/s with zig-zags, hover 1.5–3 s per flower, 3–6 visits; rabbits avoid within 45 units.

“Units” are rabbit-scaled pixels: on the 1920-wide slideshow stage one unit is 2 px.
