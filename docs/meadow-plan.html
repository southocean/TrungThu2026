Rabbit Meadow Plan

How the slideshow's jade rabbit and its little world grow over a long afternoon of looping, so the slides are readable at first and stay fresh later. Everything on the timeline below is built; tweak the numbers here and in PHASES in slides.js.

The idea

Complexity is a distraction, but a welcome one once people are bored of the slides. It starts with just the rabbit and adds one thing at a time as total play time grows. The first ten minutes stay quiet so people actually read the slides.

Phases follow total play time: only time with autoplay running counts. Play 10 min, pause 1 min, play 20 min = 30 min. The clock is kept in the browser per day, so reloading the page on the day carries on where it was; a new day starts from zero.

Note that a new element appears gently: grass takes about 2.5 min to grow in, flowers take 12–18 s to bloom, the first friend visit comes 20–45 s after its phase starts.

Phase-in timeline

Minutes of total play time. Gold = the rabbits and visitors, green = the ecosystem, dashed = ideas not built yet.

  1. 0

    Just the rabbit built

    Hops in from a side on the first play. Rests, grooms, sniffs, stands up to look around, short hops toward open space (never more than 6 in a row), strolls, sploots, naps with z's. Digs up a carrot on the spot every 1–2 min and eats it. No butterflies, no binkies yet.

  2. 10

    A butterfly built

    One round-winged butterfly at a time, roughly every couple of minutes, flitting around the rabbit. The rabbit watches, hops after it and binkies when it dips low. Binkies unlock.

  3. 15

    A friend visits built

    A smaller cream rabbit with brown ears and a brown back patch hops in from the nearer side every 9–12 min and stays 1–1.7 min. The two touch noses, stay near each other, play follow-the-leader, groom each other or sploot side by side, then the friend hops off.

  4. 25

    More butterflies built

    Up to 2 at once, about twice as often.

  5. 40

    Friends share built

    When one rabbit pulls up a carrot the other may hop over and nibble the other end; it goes 1.8× faster. Visits every 6–9 min, lasting 1.5–2.5 min.

  6. 60

    Grass grows in built

    Low grass patches rise out of the floor a few blades at a time over about 2.5 minutes.

  7. 70

    Carrots grow in the ground built

    Digging on the spot stops. One carrot at a time sprouts among the grass and ripens over 50 s; a rabbit that sees a ripe one (within 260 units) goes and digs it up.

  8. 80

    First flowers built

    Up to 2 flowers: sprout, bud, bloom about a minute, wilt, gone. Butterflies now look for blooms, land, sip and move on; rabbits sometimes smell a flower.

  9. 88

    More flowers built

    Up to 4 flowers and 2 carrots in the ground.

  10. 95

    A bee built

    One bee: fast zig-zags, hovers over flowers instead of landing. A rabbit flattens its ears and hops away if it buzzes at its face.

  11. 110

    Full meadow built

    Up to 8 flowers (fewer on narrow screens), 3 butterflies, 2 bees; the friend visits every 4–6 min.

  12. 130+

    Later surprises ideas

    Fireflies around the flowers while a rabbit naps · falling autumn leaves · a short rain shower that speeds up growth · a dropped mooncake to nibble · a slow snail the rabbit sniffs.

Rules that keep it calm

  • Everything lives in the bottom strip of the stage; insects stay within about 160 rabbit-units of the floor.
  • Caps scale with stage width, so a phone never gets crowded.
  • Pause freezes the ground exactly, insects fly off for a clean slide, and the play-time clock stops.
  • The site's membership-box rabbit stays at the base level (rabbit, carrots, the odd butterfly), with no phases and no friend.

What's in the code

rabbit.js is one file for every rabbit on the site and the slideshow. The world (grass, flowers, carrots in the ground, insects) is shared; each rabbit is its own Bunny with its own state, cooldowns and look.

ElementBehaviour and numbers
RabbitRest fidgets (blink, glance, ears, groom, sniff, stand up, loaf); hops with crouch, arc and landing squash; max 6 hops then rest; strolls remember their goal; sploot every 20–40 s at most; nap 8–15 s every 1.5–2.5 min with a stretch; binky every 15–35 s at most. The roll-over is gone.
Friend rabbit86% size, cream fur, brown ears and back patch. Arrives from the nearer side, greets with a 2.4 s nose touch, then plays every 14–24 s at most: follow-the-leader (½ of the time), grooming, side-by-side sploot. Shares carrots when share is on (65% of the time, within 320 units).
Carrots on the spotDig 1.4–2 s, pull 0.9 s, eat 5.5 s (1.8× faster when shared), 10–20 s between. On the site, grabbing mid-meal leaves the carrot on the floor (up to 5).
ButterfliesRound wings (pointed and swallowtail still drawn in code), six colours. Without flowers they flit around the rabbit; with flowers they seek blooms, sip 3–7 s, visit 2–4, leave after 30–55 s. A rabbit chases one at a time for 8–14 s, switching every 4–7 s.
GrassPatches every ~150 units, 26–70 wide, blades 5–14 high, sway; ~22% of blades in front of the rabbits. Grows in over 150 s.
FlowersDaisy, pink daisy, star, tulip, bellflower. Grow 12–18 s, bloom 45–75 s, wilt 7–10 s.
Carrots in the groundRipen in 50 s, a new one every 20–35 s while there's room, spotted within 260 units.
BeesNeed blooms. 150 units/s with zig-zags, hover 1.5–3 s per flower, 3–6 visits; rabbits avoid within 45 units.

“Units” are rabbit-scaled pixels: on the 1920-wide slideshow stage one unit is 2 px.
